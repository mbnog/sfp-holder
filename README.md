# sfp-holder
3D-printable holders for SFPs and QSFPs.  Mount these facing upward, or on an angle, or sideways.  Mounting these downward and expecting them to hold your optics may result in severe dissapointment...

Obviously these are not designed for high-density situations.  These are designed to hold SFPs that are tracked as individual inventory items; there's enough room for a human with a handheld barcode scanner to read the barcodes in any row.
